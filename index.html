SideBySideCapital Automation - Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Last updated: October 2026

Application Overview

SideBySideCapital Publisher is an internal automation tool used solely to render and upload financial comparison data videos to the authorized SideBySideCapital YouTube channel.

Data Access & Use

Our application requests access to the YouTube Data API v3 (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload) strictly to upload video content directly created by our publishing pipeline. We do not access, store, process, or sell any personal user data, viewer information, or third-party Google account data.

Data Retention & Deletion

No user data is retained or shared with external parties. Access tokens are stored locally in secured environment files and can be revoked at any time via Google Account Security settings.
